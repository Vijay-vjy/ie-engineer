What I do

Industrial engineering that shows up in the numbers

From the first time study to the daily P&L — practical systems that make lines faster, leaner and easier to plan.

SAM / SMV Engineering

Time study, method study and work study to set accurate standard minutes for every style and operation.

Line Balancing

Balance operations and manpower to hit output targets with the fewest bottlenecks and idle minutes.

5S, Kaizen, Value Stream Mapping and Poka-Yoke to remove waste from the shop floor.
